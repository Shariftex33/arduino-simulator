How to use

  1. Press + on the canvas, search, and add a component. × on the board hides the board.
  2. Drag from a pin or breadboard hole and drop on another to connect a wire. Holes in the same column (a–e or f–j) are tied together; power rails are separate. ⚡ Auto Wire seats parts and lands wires on the holes.
  3. Scroll to zoom the canvas. Drag empty space to pan. Press 100% to restore the view.
  4. Run the simulation with the green play icon (Ctrl+Enter). Drag the canvas and code edges to resize.
  5. Undo / Redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y) restores components, wires, the board, and code.
  6. Click a button component, or hold Space to press it.
  7. Moving a sensor slider changes its value while the simulation is running.
  8. Your work is saved in the browser automatically. You can also download JSON, .ino, or a PNG (with or without the canvas background) from the File menu. Right-click the canvas for the same image options. A selection exports just those parts.
