    Content guidelines

    1. Intro. Use a specific title and one sentence that says what the build does.
    2. The team. Name the people who made it. If this is not your project, say so.
    3. Devices & Components. List the boards, sensors, and modules, with quantities.
    4. Hardware & Tools. Add the tools you actually used.
    5. Software & Tools. Name the apps and platforms, including this simulator if you used it.
    6. Project description. Explain the wiring, the behavior, and the first thing to try.
    7. Code. Share a link or paste the sketch.
    8. Arduino App Lab. Attach a .zip only when the project is an Arduino App.
    9. Downloadable files. Diagrams, models, or datasheets belong here.
    10. Documentation. Extra write-ups or videos go here.
